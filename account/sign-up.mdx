Getting started with askROI takes just a few clicks. Choose the sign-in method that works best for you, complete a short setup flow, and you'll land in your workspace with 2,000 free monthly credits ready to use — no credit card required.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to askROI">
    Open [https://askroi.com](https://askroi.com) in your browser, or download the askROI app on iOS or Android. Tap or click **Get Started** to open the account creation screen.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose your sign-in method">
    Select the option that suits you best:

    - **Google** — Sign in with your existing Google account. Your Google profile name and email are used to create your askROI account instantly.
    - **Apple** — Sign in with Apple ID. You can choose to share or hide your email address using Apple's private relay feature.
    - **Microsoft** — Sign in with any Microsoft or Outlook account, including work and school accounts that use Microsoft Entra ID.
    - **Email** — Enter your email address and follow the verification link sent to your inbox to complete sign-up without a third-party account.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete account setup">
    After authenticating, askROI walks you through a brief onboarding flow. Set your display name and confirm your preferences. This helps askROI adapt to your writing tone and workflow right from the start.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Start with 2,000 free monthly credits">
    Your account is ready. Every new account on the Free plan starts with **2,000 credits per month**, which refresh automatically. Use them for AI chat, web research, image generation, file analysis, and more.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Sign In to an Existing Account

If you already have an askROI account, navigate to [https://askroi.com](https://askroi.com) and click **Sign In**. Select the same sign-in method you used when you created your account — Google, Apple, Microsoft, or email — and you'll be taken directly to your workspace. Your chat history, Projects, and settings sync automatically across web, iOS, and Android.

<Note>
  askROI never stores your password directly. When you sign in with Google, Apple, or Microsoft, authentication is handled entirely by that provider. If you signed up with email, askROI sends a secure verification link each time you log in — no password to remember or reset.
</Note>
