Your askROI account settings give you full control over your profile, billing plan, and connected sign-in methods. Access them any time by clicking your avatar or profile icon in the top-right corner of the app, then selecting **Settings**.

## Account Profile

Keep your profile accurate so askROI can personalise your experience and surface the right defaults across chat and writing.

- **Display name** — Click your name in the Profile section and type a new one. Your display name appears in Group Chats and shared Projects.
- **Email address** — If you signed up with email, you can update your address under **Profile → Email**. A verification link is sent to the new address before the change takes effect.
- **Preferences** — Adjust your default language, theme (light or dark), notification settings, and writing-tone preferences from the **Preferences** tab. These settings sync across web, iOS, and Android automatically.

## Changing Your Plan

Upgrade to Pro or adjust your subscription directly inside askROI — no need to contact sales for self-serve changes.

1. Open **Settings → Billing & Plan**.
2. Review your current plan and credit balance.
3. Click **Upgrade to Pro** to move to the $10/month plan with 10,000 monthly credits, or click **Downgrade to Free** if you're on Pro and want to return to the Free tier.
4. Follow the on-screen prompts to confirm payment details when upgrading.

Your new credit allowance takes effect at the start of your next billing cycle. If you upgrade mid-cycle, Pro credits are applied immediately and prorated billing is reflected on your next invoice.

<Tip>
  Want to explore Pro features before committing? Compare everything that's included on the [Plans & Pricing](/plans-and-pricing) page.
</Tip>

## Connected Sign-In Methods

askROI uses your chosen identity provider — Google, Apple, or Microsoft — to authenticate you securely. You can review which provider is linked to your account under **Settings → Security → Connected Accounts**.

- **Adding a connection** — If your account was created with email, you can link a Google, Apple, or Microsoft account as an additional sign-in option.
- **Removing a connection** — Click **Disconnect** next to any provider you no longer want associated with your account. Make sure at least one sign-in method remains active before disconnecting.
- **Switching providers** — To change your primary sign-in method, add the new provider first, verify it works, then remove the old one.

## Deleting Your Account

To permanently delete your askROI account, go to **Settings → Account → Delete Account** and follow the confirmation steps. Before deleting, consider exporting your chat history and any files stored in your Projects — exports are available from the **Projects** section of Settings.

<Warning>
  Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. All your chat history, Projects, uploaded files, and saved preferences are erased immediately. askROI cannot recover this data after deletion is confirmed.
</Warning>

If you'd rather pause your usage than delete your account, you can downgrade to the Free plan at any time to stop billing without losing your data.
