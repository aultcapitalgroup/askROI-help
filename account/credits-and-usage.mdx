askROI uses a simple credit system to measure your monthly usage across every feature — from AI chat and web research to image generation and file analysis. Understanding how credits work helps you get the most out of your plan and avoid any unexpected interruptions to your workflow.

## What Are Credits?

Credits are the universal unit of usage in askROI. Every action that draws on AI processing — sending a chat message, running a web search, generating an image, or analysing a file — consumes a small number of credits from your monthly allowance. When your credits refresh at the start of each billing cycle, you get a full allocation to spend however you like across the platform.

Credits give you flexibility: you're not locked into separate quotas for chat versus images versus search. One pool of credits covers everything, so you can focus on the work that matters most to you.

## How Credits Are Used

Different activities draw on different amounts of credits depending on the complexity of the task. Here's a guide to roughly what to expect:

| Activity | Approximate Credit Cost |
|---|---|
| AI chat message | Low — a few credits per exchange |
| Real-time web search | Low–moderate — varies by depth of results |
| File upload and analysis (PDF, spreadsheet, doc) | Moderate — scales with file size and complexity |
| Image generation | Moderate–high — per image created |
| Image editing | Moderate — per edit operation |
| Voice input processing | Low — per session |
| Agentic task execution | Variable — scales with steps completed |
| Code assistance and debugging | Low–moderate — per interaction |

<Note>
  Credit costs are approximate and may vary as askROI improves its models and features. The most up-to-date usage information is always available inside the app under **Settings → Usage**.
</Note>

## Checking Your Usage

You can check your remaining credits and a breakdown of recent activity at any time:

1. Open askROI on web, iOS, or Android.
2. Tap or click your **profile avatar** in the top-right corner.
3. Select **Settings → Usage**.

The usage screen shows your current credit balance, total credits for the billing period, and a history of how your credits have been spent across different features. Your usage syncs in real time across all your devices.

## When Credits Run Out

If you exhaust your monthly credits before your billing cycle resets, askROI will let you know with an in-app notification. At that point:

- **Free plan users** — Core features pause until your credits refresh at the start of the next billing month. You can upgrade to Pro at any time to immediately receive a fresh 10,000-credit allocation.
- **Pro plan users** — You'll be notified when your balance is low so you can plan accordingly. Contact [sales@askroi.com](mailto:sales@askroi.com) or call **877-427-5764** if you need to discuss higher-volume options.

Upgrading to Pro is the fastest way to restore full access and unlock a significantly larger monthly allowance.

<Info>
  Credits reset automatically on your billing date each month — you don't need to take any action. Unused credits do not roll over to the following month.
</Info>
