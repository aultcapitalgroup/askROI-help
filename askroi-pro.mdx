Pro members can use askROI's unlimited mode in chat, which uses no credits for basic prompts.

Pro membership includes 10,000 monthly credits for greater access to premium AI models, image generation, video generation, audio generation, and higher-volume usage.

Pro members can also purchase additional credit packs as needed.

## How can I subscribe?

### Steps for desktop users

Log into your account and follow the steps below:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Settings or click Upgrade at the top of askROI">
    Click **Settings** or your profile icon in the bottom right corner, then click **Upgrade to Pro**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete checkout on the Stripe billing page">
    If you have a promotion code, you can add it here. Enter your payment information and click **Subscribe**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Steps for iOS / Android users

Log into the app and follow the steps below:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap your profile icon in the top right corner of the app">
    Tap **Subscribe to Pro**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use Apple or Google Pay to process the transaction">
    Confirm the purchase to activate your Pro subscription.
  </Step>
</Steps>
