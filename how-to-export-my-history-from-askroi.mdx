Here you can find out how to export your Chat History within askROI after the upgrade to askROI 2.0.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in">
    Log in to askROI through the app or website.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Click **Account** and then **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export Legacy Data">
    Click **Export Legacy Data** at the bottom of the **Settings** page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your email">
    Check your email for a link to download your Chat History.

    <Note>
      Please check your Spam/Junk folder if it does not show up within your Inbox.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your data">
    Once you download your data History, you can save it to a folder on your PC or phone.
  </Step>
</Steps>
