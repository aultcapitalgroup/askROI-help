Keep your contact information current in askROI by updating your email address. You can also add additional emails and change which one is primary.

## Updating Your Email in askROI

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click your Profile Icon (Settings)">
    Select **Settings** from the pop-up menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Account">
    Click **Profile**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update your email">
    Update the **Email address** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save changes">
    Press **Update**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add an additional email address

1. Click **Security Settings** within the **Settings** page.
2. Click **Add email address** under the Profile tab.
3. Enter an email address and click the **Add** button to save it.

## Remove an existing email address

1. First, add a new email address.
2. Then, click the three dots next to the email you want to delete and select **Remove Email**.

## Change a secondary email to your primary email

1. Click the three dots next to the email you want to set as primary.
2. Select **Set as primary**.

Your email is now updated!
