Though we hate to see you go, we understand that sometimes it's time to move on. If you need to delete your askROI account, follow these simple steps.

## Steps to Delete Your Account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Account">
    Find **Account** at the bottom left side of the screen and click on it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Click **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Security Settings">
    Open **Security Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Security tab">
    Click the **Security** tab to access your security settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find Delete account">
    In the security settings, locate the **Delete account** link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Delete account">
    If you're sure you're ready to part ways, click **Delete account**. A pop-up will appear to confirm you understand this is permanent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm deletion">
    If you're absolutely sure, type **Delete account** in the text box and click the red **Delete Account** button to confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  After confirming, your account will be permanently deleted, so make sure you've saved any important info beforehand. There's no going back.
</Warning>
