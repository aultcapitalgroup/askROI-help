Though we hate to see you go, we understand that sometimes it's time to move on. If you need to delete your askROI account, follow these simple steps.

## Steps to Delete Your Account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click your Profile Icon (Settings)">
    Select **Settings** from the pop-up menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Account Management">
    Click Delete Account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The Delete Account pop-up will appear">
    If you're sure you're ready to part ways, click **Delete account.**
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  After confirming, your account will be permanently deleted, so make sure you've saved any important info beforehand. There's no going back.
</Warning>
