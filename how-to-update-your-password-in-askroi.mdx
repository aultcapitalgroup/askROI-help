Regularly changing your password is a key part of keeping your askROI account secure. This article walks you through updating your password and adding two-step verification.

## Updating Your Password in askROI and adding Two-step Verification

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click your Profile Icon (Settings)">
    Select **Settings** from the pop-up menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Security">
    Open **Security Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the Security tab">
    Click the Security tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Update Password">
    You'll be asked to enter your current password and create a new one and confirm it.

    Make sure your new password is strong, unique, and secure.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your changes">
    Click **Save** to save your changes. You'll receive confirmation that your password has been updated.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**You can also add two-step verification here.**

- Click **Add two-step verification** and follow the steps outlined.
