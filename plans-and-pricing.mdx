## Understanding Credits

Credits are the shared currency for everything you do inside askROI. Every interaction — whether it's a chat message, a web search, an image generation, or a file analysis — draws from your monthly credit balance.

Here's how credits work:

- **Monthly refresh** — your full credit balance resets on the same date each month, regardless of how many you used
- **AI chat** — each message exchange uses credits based on the length and complexity of the request
- **Web searches** — searching the web in real time draws a small number of credits per query
- **Image generation** — generating or editing an image uses more credits than a standard chat message
- **File analysis** — uploading and analyzing a document consumes credits based on the size and depth of the analysis

<Info>
  Not all interactions cost the same number of credits. A short chat reply uses fewer credits than a deep research session or a high-resolution image generation. You can always check your remaining balance in your account settings.
</Info>

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What happens when I run out of credits?">
    When your monthly credits are exhausted, you won't be able to start new credit-consuming actions until your balance refreshes at the start of your next billing period. You'll receive a notification as your balance gets low so you're never caught off guard. If you regularly hit your limit, upgrading to Pro gives you 10,000 credits per month — five times the Free allowance.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I upgrade or downgrade at any time?">
    Yes. You can upgrade from Free to Pro or downgrade from Pro to Free at any time from your account settings. Upgrades take effect immediately and your new credit balance is applied right away. Downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing period so you keep your Pro benefits until the cycle ends.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is there a free trial for Pro?">
    The Free plan itself serves as your no-commitment way to explore askROI — you get 2,000 credits every month with no credit card required. If you want to experience Pro-level limits before committing, reach out to the sales team at [sales@askroi.com](mailto:sales@askroi.com) or call **877-427-5764** to ask about trial options.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do unused credits roll over to the next month?">
    No — credits do not roll over. Your balance resets to your plan's full monthly allowance (2,000 for Free, 10,000 for Pro) at the start of each new billing period. This ensures every user starts each month with their full allocation.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
