Getting started with askROI takes just a few minutes — no credit card, no setup headaches. Follow the steps below to create your account, send your first message, and start exploring the full workspace.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Head to [askroi.com](https://askroi.com) and choose how you want to sign in. askROI supports four sign-in methods:

    - **Google** — sign in with your existing Google account
    - **Apple** — use Sign in with Apple for a private, fast setup
    - **Microsoft** — connect with your Microsoft or Outlook account
    - **Email** — register with any email address and a password of your choice

    Pick the option that works best for you and complete the short onboarding flow. Your free account is ready instantly — no waiting, no credit card required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start your first chat">
    Once you're in, type any message in the chat input and press **Enter** (or tap **Send** on mobile). Try a question, a writing prompt, or a task you've been putting off.

    Before you send, notice the toolbar above the input. You can enhance any message by enabling one or more tools:

    - **Web Search** — pulls real-time results with citations into your response
    - **File Upload** — attach a PDF, spreadsheet, or document and ask questions about it
    - **Image Generation** — describe a visual and askROI creates it on the spot

    You can mix and match tools in the same conversation — ask a question, upload a file for context, and generate an image, all in one thread.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a Project">
    Projects are organized workspaces that persist across sessions. Unlike a one-off chat, a Project remembers the files you've uploaded, the context you've built up, and the history of everything you've discussed — so you can pick up right where you left off.

    To create your first Project:

    1. Click **Projects** in the left sidebar (or tap the **Projects** tab on mobile).
    2. Select **New Project** and give it a name that reflects what you're working on.
    3. Add any files, notes, or context you want the AI to keep in mind throughout the project.
    4. Start chatting — every message inside the Project builds on everything that came before it.

    Use Projects for ongoing work like content campaigns, research briefs, codebases, or client deliverables.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore the full tool suite">
    Beyond chat, askROI includes a rich set of specialized tools accessible from the tool picker in the workspace. Tap the **+** or **Tools** button to browse everything available:

    - **Draw & Design** — sketch ideas or build vector graphics with AI-assisted design tools
    - **Image** — generate, edit, and remix images with natural language prompts
    - **Research** — run deep, multi-source research sessions with organized citations
    - **Video** — create and work with video content directly in the workspace
    - **Google Workspace** — connect Docs, Sheets, and Drive for seamless file integration
    - **MCP Integrations** — link 100+ third-party apps and services to extend what askROI can do
    - **Voice** — dictate messages and hear responses read back to you

    Spend a few minutes clicking through the tool picker to see what's available — you'll likely find something that fits a workflow you didn't expect.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  The **Free plan includes 2,000 credits every month** — no credit card needed. Credits refresh on your billing date each month, giving you a generous allowance for chat, search, image generation, and file analysis without spending a cent.
</Tip>
