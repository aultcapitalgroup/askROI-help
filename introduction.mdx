askROI is an AI workspace designed to handle the full range of your daily work — not just one narrow task. Instead of juggling a browser tab for research, a separate tool for writing, another for images, and yet another for file review, askROI brings everything into a single, intelligent environment that learns how you work and gets better over time.

## Everything in One Place

Most AI tools do one thing well. askROI does all of it — and keeps everything connected. From a single workspace you can:

- **Chat with AI** that adapts to your writing style and the depth of detail you prefer
- **Search the web in real time** and get answers with cited sources you can verify
- **Upload and analyze files** — PDFs, spreadsheets, Word docs — and extract insights instantly
- **Generate and edit images** without switching to a separate design app
- **Write, debug, and review code** across dozens of programming languages
- **Speak your thoughts** with voice input and listen back with voice playback
- **Create group chats** where your team shares AI context in real time
- **Run agentic tasks** that execute multi-step work on your behalf
- **Connect 100+ tools** through MCP integrations and Google Workspace

Every one of these capabilities lives inside the same workspace, so your context travels with you rather than getting lost between apps.

## Smarter AI That Adapts to You

askROI isn't a static chatbot. It observes how you communicate, learns your writing tone, and tailors the depth and format of its responses to match your workflow. The more you use it, the more it reflects the way you think.

A few things worth knowing about how askROI handles your data:

- **Remembers project context** — files, history, and notes you add to a Project persist across sessions so you never have to repeat yourself
- **Learns your tone** — whether you write formally or conversationally, askROI picks up on your style and mirrors it back
- **Adapts to your workflow** — you can shift between research mode, writing mode, and code mode without losing your thread
- **Never stores passwords** — askROI does not ask for or retain your credentials for any connected service

<Note>
  askROI v2.0 is **3× faster** than its predecessor — so you spend less time waiting and more time doing.
</Note>

## Works Everywhere

Your work doesn't stop when you close your laptop. askROI is fully available on:

- **Web** — access your full workspace at [askroi.com](https://askroi.com) from any browser
- **iOS** — the native iPhone and iPad app keeps your Projects and chat history in sync
- **Android** — the Android app gives you the same complete experience on any device

All three platforms sync automatically, so a Project you start on the web picks up exactly where you left off on your phone — and vice versa.
