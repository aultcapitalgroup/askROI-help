Projects let you organize related chats and files in askROI. This guide walks through creating a Project and running your first prompt inside it.

## Creating a Project

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Projects">
    The projects module may be hidden under the "Show More" menu. If so, click Show More and the menu will expand showing Projects.

    Click **Projects.**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a new Project">
    Click the **Create first project **button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name and create">
    Enter the Project title name and click **Create**.

    Optionally, you can assign the project a color coding by clicking the folder icon before clicking the **Create** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click your newly created Project">
    or create another project by clicking the **\+ Create** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Files and Instructions">
    Click **Project Files** to add files that will be used for this project. You may add files from your library by clicking **Add from library** or upload the files directly by clicking **Upload files**.  <br /><br />Click **Add Instructions** to modify the instructions for this project. Enter your own instructions by typing them, or speech-to-text by clicking the microphone icon (requires a microphone) and optionally enhance what instructions you enter by clicking the **Enhance** button. When finished click **Add**.

    Insert your prompt. 

    Optionally, click the **Standard** button to select **Standard** or **Premium** models, or **Model Council** if you would like to receive answers from multiple models. Toggle **Music** or **Video** if you would like your prompts to generate media.<br /><br />Click the arrow button to submit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start chatting">
    Once the project is created, optional instructions and files are added you can then start chatting.
  </Step>
</Steps>
