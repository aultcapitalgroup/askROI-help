Projects let you organize related chats and files in askROI. This guide walks through creating a Project and running your first prompt inside it.

## Creating a Project

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Projects">
    Go to **Projects**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a new Project">
    Click the plus sign icon.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name and create">
    Enter the Project title name and click **Create**.

    ![Create Project dialog](https://help.askroi.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Dec-17-2025-08-45-17-8849-PM.png?width=539&height=238&name=image-png-Dec-17-2025-08-45-17-8849-PM.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Report Summaries folder">
    Open the **Report Summaries** folder under **Projects**.

    ![Report Summaries folder](https://help.askroi.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Dec-17-2025-08-46-28-3702-PM.png?width=411&height=120&name=image-png-Dec-17-2025-08-46-28-3702-PM.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit your prompt">
    Insert your prompt, upload any instructions needed for the prompt, select which LLM you prefer, and press the arrow button to submit.

    ![Prompt input for Project](https://help.askroi.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Dec-17-2025-08-47-02-5945-PM.png?width=616&height=123&name=image-png-Dec-17-2025-08-47-02-5945-PM.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start chatting">
    Once the Project is created, you can then request your chat.
  </Step>
</Steps>
