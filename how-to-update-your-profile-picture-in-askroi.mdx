Give your askROI account a personal touch by adding your profile picture. This guide shows you how to update it from Profile settings.

## How to Update

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click your Profile Icon (Settings)">
    Select **Settings** from the pop-up menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Account">
    The **Account** tab should be open by default.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Profile">
    Click **Profile**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add or change your image">
    Tap the picture section to add or change an image. Retrieve the image from the file and press **Open**.

    or click **Remove Photo** to remove the photo without uploading a new one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update">
    Click **Update** to save your changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

That's it! Your new profile picture is now visible to others.
