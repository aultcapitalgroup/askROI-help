---

Groups are shared chats. Use them to collaborate with a group or team in the same thread.

## Create a Group

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Groups">
    In the left menu, click **Show more**, then **Groups**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the Group">
    Click **\+ New Group Chat **(or **Create first group** if you do not have any yet). Enter a group chat name and click **Create**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share the Group Chat">
    When you first open a new group chat, a shareable link will be provided. Anyone with the link can join your chat.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manage the Group">
    You can manage the group from the menu at the top of the chat page. Click the drop-down menu to:

    -  Invite members 
    - Rename the group 
    - Customize the chat (add instructions)
    - See current members and remove or ban them
    - Access the group chats library (a separate library just for this group)
    - Search the chat
    - Catch up with unread messages
    - Delete the group
  </Step>
  <Step title="Utilize AI in a Group Chat">
    To utilize askROI in a group chat be sure to first type **@askROI** before your query.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Members can see anything posted in the group chat and library.
