## 4. Health Data (Apple Health and Health Connect)

If you choose to connect Apple Health on iOS or Health Connect on Android and grant permission, askROI may collect, access, and process health and fitness data from your device, including sleep data, step count, heart rate, resting heart rate, and active calories burned.

We use this data only to provide the health-related features you request in the app, including answering your questions and generating summaries, recent readings, and trends about your activity, sleep, and heart rate.

Health data access is permission-based. askROI requests access only after you choose to enable these features and approve the request in the app. You can revoke access at any time in Apple Health or Health Connect settings on your device.

We do not collect health data in the background and only access it when you initiate a request within the app.

When you request a health-related response, relevant health data may be read from your device and securely transmitted to askROI so we can process your request and return a response in the chat. This may include processing by our vendors and service providers, including contracted LLM providers, solely for the purpose of providing the Services to you.

We do not sell health data and do not use health data for advertising. We do not use health data for marketing, profiling, insurance, or credit purposes. Health data accessed through Apple HealthKit is used solely to provide and improve app functionality and is not used for advertising or shared with third parties for advertising purposes.

Health data that is processed as part of your use of the Services may be retained as part of your chat history and account records for as long as needed to provide the Services, resolve disputes, enforce our agreements, maintain safety and security, and comply with legal obligations. You may request deletion of your Personal Information, including stored health-related data contained in your account or chat history, through your askROI account where available or by contacting us at datap@askroi.com.

We implement commercially reasonable technical, administrative, and organizational safeguards designed to protect health data from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction.

## 5. Google user data {#google-user-data}

This section applies when you connect a Google account to askROI, including Gmail, Google Drive, and Google Calendar. If another part of this Privacy Policy would allow a broader use of Google user data, this section controls.

The use of information received from Google Workspace scopes will adhere to the [Google User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the [Limited Use requirements](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy#additional_requirements_for_specific_api_scopes).

**What we access.** You choose which Google products to connect.

- Gmail: messages and threads, including their content, sender, recipients, subject, date, and labels. We see the names, types, and sizes of attachments, but not their contents. You can also have askROI save drafts and send email.
- Google Drive: the files you can access, and the files askROI creates for you.
- Google Calendar: your calendar list, your availability, and your events, including the events you ask askROI to create or change.
- Your Google account: your name, email address, and basic profile.

**How we use it.** We use Google user data only to provide and improve the features you can see in askROI.

- To answer your requests, such as searching your mail, summarizing a thread, or finding a time to meet.
- To save drafts and send email. A draft stays in your Gmail and reaches no one, so askROI saves it without asking. askROI sends a message only after you approve that message.
- Only if you turn them on for that account:
  - Work on its own: your agent can read the account in work it starts itself.
  - Use in routines: routines that name the account can read it and start when something new arrives. For Gmail, we regularly check for new mail and read its sender, subject, and preview.
  - Learn from your email: about once a week, your agent reads a small set of recent mail and suggests facts to remember. Nothing is saved until you accept it. Each pass appears in your agent's activity.

**What we do not do.**

- We do not sell Google user data or transfer it to advertising platforms, data brokers, or information resellers.
- We do not use it to serve ads, including retargeting and personalized or interest-based ads.
- We do not use it to determine credit-worthiness or for lending.
- We do not use it to create, train, or improve any AI or machine learning model, beyond personalizing your own agent, such as the facts you accept.
- We do not build an index or a permanent copy of your mailbox.

**Who receives it.** We share Google user data only as needed for the feature you are using.

| Recipient | Why | What they receive |
| --- | --- | --- |
| OpenRouter | Routes each request to an AI model | The request, which can include your Google data |
| TypeSafe and Perplexity | Run the small models that route and sort requests | The text they decide on |
| Convex | Database, backend, and file storage | Your chats, tool results, and encrypted access tokens |
| Cloudflare | Stores files you or your agent create | Those files |
| E2B | Run code in isolated workspaces when a task needs it | What the task places there |
| Serper | Web search, when your agent searches | The search queries your agent writes |
| fal, ElevenLabs, and Fish Audio | Make images and audio you ask for | The prompt, or the text to read aloud |

Before a chat holding Google data sends it to a new website or connected app, askROI asks you first. It also asks before you share such a chat, because its answers can quote that data.

We may also disclose Google user data when needed for security, such as investigating abuse, or to comply with applicable law. We transfer it in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets only after you give explicit prior consent.

**How long we keep it.**

- Answers, summaries, and other text your agent wrote stay in your chats until you delete those chats or your account. They can quote your Google data.
- Facts and suggestions drawn from Gmail stay until you delete them or remove the connection.
- Access tokens are encrypted at rest and deleted when you remove the connection.

**Your controls.**

- Turn off any permission above in your askROI integration settings.
- Remove a connection there. We delete its access tokens, its routine previews, and the facts and suggestions that came from it, and we offer to delete the chats holding its content. If another connected account of the same Google product remains, those facts and suggestions stay until you remove that one too.
- We ask Google to revoke askROI's access when you remove the last askROI connection for that Google account, because Google treats those connections as one grant. You can also remove access at any time in your Google Account.
- Drafts saved in your Gmail stay there until you delete them.
- Deleting your askROI account deletes your connections, chats, and saved facts. You can also write to datap@askROI.com.

For instructions on managing and deleting this data, see [Manage and delete connected Google account data](/google-data-controls).

## 6. Data processing and artificial intelligence ("AI") services

askROI provides AI-powered services through a combination of licensed Large Language Models (LLMs) and intelligent routing. Here's how we handle your data in this context:

**Licensed LLM Services:** We partner with leading LLM providers to process your queries. When you use our Services, your input may be sent to one or more of these licensed LLM services.

**Intelligent Routing:** We use an intelligent routing system to direct your queries to the most appropriate LLM based on the content and context of your request. This routing process may involve analyzing your query, but this analysis is used solely for the purpose of selecting the appropriate LLM.

**Data Handling:** While we do not own or train the LLMs used in our Services, we are committed to protecting your data: We do not store the content of your interactions with the LLMs beyond what is necessary to provide and improve our Services. We have agreements in place with our LLM providers that restrict their use of your data to providing the LLM services.

**No Independent AI Training:** Unlike companies that own their LLMs, we do not use your data to train AI models. Your interactions are used solely to provide you with the requested services and to improve our routing and integration processes.

**Transparency:** We are committed to being transparent about our use of licensed LLMs. If you have questions about which LLM processed a particular query, you can contact us at privacy@askROI.com.

A note about accuracy: Services like askROI generate responses by reading a user's request and, in response, predicting the words most likely to appear next. In some cases, the words most likely to appear next may not be the most factually accurate. For this reason, you should not rely on the factual accuracy of output from our models. If you notice that askROI output contains factually inaccurate information about you and you would like us to correct the inaccuracy, you may submit a correction request to datap@askroi.com.
