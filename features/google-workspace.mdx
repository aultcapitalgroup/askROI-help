The askROI Google Workspace integration brings your Google files and email into your AI workspace. Once connected, you can search your Drive, analyze Docs and Sheets, and reference Gmail messages — all without leaving askROI. Ask questions about your files, generate summaries, extract data from spreadsheets, or draft replies to emails, and let askROI do the heavy lifting.

## What's Included

The Google Workspace integration gives askROI access to the following Google services:

- **Google Drive** — Search and retrieve files stored in your Drive, including documents, spreadsheets, presentations, and PDFs.
- **Google Docs** — Read, summarize, and analyze the content of your documents. Ask the AI to extract key points, rewrite sections, or answer questions based on a Doc's content.
- **Google Sheets** — Query, analyze, and visualize data from your spreadsheets. Ask the AI to identify trends, perform calculations, or produce a written summary of a Sheet.
- **Gmail** — Read and search your emails. Use askROI to summarize threads, find specific messages, and draft replies based on the context of a conversation.

## Connecting Google Workspace

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Integrations">
    In askROI, click the **Integrations** icon in the sidebar or navigate to **Settings → Integrations** to open the integrations library.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Google Workspace">
    Find **Google Workspace** in the integrations list — it's listed under the Productivity category. Click **Connect** to begin the authorization process.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize with your Google account">
    You'll be redirected to Google's sign-in page. Choose the Google account you want to connect and review the permissions askROI is requesting. Click **Allow** to grant access. You'll be returned to askROI, and Google Workspace will appear as an active integration.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Working with Google Files

After connecting, your Google Drive is available directly in askROI chat. You can reference your files by name, ask the AI to search for them, or browse your Drive from within the chat interface.

To work with a specific file, mention it in your message — for example, "Summarize the Q3 report from my Drive" or "What are the key metrics in the Sales Pipeline sheet?" askROI will locate the file, read its contents, and respond with AI-powered analysis.

You can also upload Google files alongside other documents for combined analysis. For example, attach a Google Doc and a PDF together and ask askROI to compare their contents or merge information from both sources into a single summary.

For Gmail, ask natural-language questions like "Find emails from last week about the product launch" or "Draft a reply to the latest message from Sarah." askROI reads the relevant thread and crafts a response that fits the conversation.

<Note>
  Make sure you connect the correct Google account — the one that contains the Drive files and Gmail inbox you want to work with. If you need to switch accounts, go to **Settings → Integrations**, disconnect Google Workspace, and reconnect with the right account.
</Note>
