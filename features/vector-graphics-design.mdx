askROI includes vector graphics and design tools that let you create scalable artwork, build visual concepts, and get AI-powered design feedback without needing a separate design application. Whether you're mocking up a logo, exploring a color palette, or iterating on a layout idea, you can do it all inside your workspace.

## Vector Graphics

Use askROI's vector tools to create and edit resolution-independent graphics directly in your workspace. Vector artwork scales cleanly to any size — from a small app icon to a large banner — making it ideal for logos, icons, diagrams, and illustrations.

**What you can do with vector graphics in askROI:**

- Draw and edit shapes, paths, and curves with precision controls
- Apply fills, strokes, gradients, and opacity to individual elements
- Group, layer, and arrange objects to build complex compositions
- Ask askROI to generate a vector layout or icon based on a text description, then refine it visually

Because your vector work lives inside askROI, you can reference it in chat — ask the AI to suggest modifications, explain design choices, or generate complementary assets alongside your existing artwork.

## Design Assistance

askROI's AI can act as a design collaborator, helping you make decisions about color, layout, and visual style at any stage of your project.

**Color palettes** — Describe a mood, brand, or aesthetic and askROI will suggest a harmonious color palette with hex codes you can use immediately. Ask for accessible combinations, seasonal themes, or palettes that pair well with a specific primary color.

**Layouts and composition** — Share a rough idea or a screenshot and ask askROI to critique the layout, suggest hierarchy improvements, or propose an alternative structure. You can work through multiple iterations in the same conversation.

**Design concepts** — Describe what you're building — a landing page, a pitch deck, a logo — and askROI will offer visual direction, reference styles, and explain the reasoning behind each suggestion.

<Tip>
  Combine vector tools with [image generation](/features/image-generation) for a complete creative workflow: generate a raster concept image first, then use the vector tools to recreate or refine it as a scalable graphic.
</Tip>

## Accessing the Design Tools

The vector and design tools are available from the toolbar inside any chat or Project.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a chat or Project">
    Navigate to an existing conversation or create a new one. Design tools are available in both contexts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the toolbar">
    Look for the toolbar at the bottom of the chat input area. Click the **Draw / Vector** icon to open the vector canvas.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start creating">
    Use the shape, path, and text tools on the canvas to build your graphic. You can switch back to the chat at any time to ask askROI for design suggestions or AI-generated elements.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your work">
    Save your design to a [Project](/features/projects) to keep it accessible alongside your other files, prompts, and conversation history.
  </Step>
</Steps>
