askROI's image generation lets you turn a text description into a visual in seconds. Whether you need a concept illustration, a social media graphic, or a creative piece, you can generate, iterate, and save images without ever leaving your workspace.

## Creating an Image

To generate your first image, open a chat and describe what you want to see. Then select the **Image** tool from the toolbar before sending your message. askROI will process your prompt and return a generated image directly in the conversation.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a chat">
    Start a new chat or open an existing one from your dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe your image">
    Type a prompt describing the image you want — include subjects, style, colors, and mood for the best results.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the Image tool">
    Click the **Image** icon in the chat toolbar to activate image generation mode.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send your prompt">
    Press **Send**. askROI generates your image and displays it inline in the chat.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Editing Images

Once an image is generated, you can refine it through follow-up prompts in the same conversation. askROI retains the context of your previous request, so you can make targeted adjustments without starting over.

To edit a generated image, reply in the chat with a description of the change you want — for example, *"Make the background darker"* or *"Change the style to watercolor."* askROI will produce an updated version based on your feedback. You can iterate as many times as needed until the result matches your vision.

<Info>
  Each follow-up prompt counts as a new image generation toward your monthly credit usage.
</Info>

## Using Images in Projects

Save any generated image to a [Project](/features/projects) to keep it organized alongside your other files, notes, and context. Inside a Project, your images persist across sessions so you can reference or share them at any time.

To save an image, hover over the generated image in the chat and select **Save to Project**. Choose an existing Project or create a new one. Your image will appear in the Project's file library, ready to use in future conversations within that workspace.

<Note>
  Pro users can generate significantly more images per month than Free users. If you reach your monthly image limit on the Free plan, consider upgrading to Pro for higher usage. See the [pricing page](/plans-and-pricing) for a full comparison.
</Note>

<Tip>
  **Write better prompts for better images.** The more specific your description, the more accurate the result:

  - **Describe the subject clearly** — *"a golden retriever sitting in a sunlit meadow"* beats *"a dog outside."*
  - **Specify an art style** — watercolor, photorealistic, flat illustration, oil painting, pixel art.
  - **Include color and mood** — *"muted earth tones, calm and peaceful atmosphere."*
  - **Mention composition** — *"wide-angle shot," "close-up portrait," "top-down view."*
</Tip>
