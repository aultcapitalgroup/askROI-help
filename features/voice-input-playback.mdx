askROI lets you interact entirely by voice — speak a prompt to send it, then listen to the AI's response read back to you. Whether you're on the go, away from your keyboard, or just prefer to think out loud, voice makes askROI faster and more natural to use across all your devices.

## Speaking to askROI

Voice input is available on all platforms — **web**, **iOS**, and **Android** — and works in any chat, including chats inside a Project.

To activate voice input:

1. Tap or click the **microphone icon** in the chat input field.
2. Speak your message clearly when the indicator shows askROI is listening.
3. Pause or tap the icon again to stop recording. Your spoken words are transcribed and sent as your message.

The transcription happens in real time, so you can watch your words appear as you speak. If a word is transcribed incorrectly, tap the text field to edit before sending.

<Note>
Voice input is available on all plans — Free and Pro — across web, iOS, and Android at no extra cost.
</Note>

## Listening to Responses

After askROI replies, you can have the response read aloud instead of reading it yourself. To play back a response:

- Click or tap the **speaker icon** on any AI message.
- The response begins playing immediately. Use the on-screen controls to **pause**, **resume**, or **stop** playback.
- Playback continues in the background if you switch tabs or lock your phone, so you can listen while doing something else.

Playback is especially useful for longer responses — summaries, reports, and explanations — where listening is faster than reading.

## Tips for Voice Use

Get the most out of voice input and playback with these practices:

- **Speak clearly and at a natural pace.** You don't need to slow down or over-enunciate — askROI handles natural speech well, including pauses and filler words that get cleaned up automatically.
- **Use voice for hands-free research.** Ask askROI to search a topic, summarize an article, or draft a paragraph while your hands are busy. The agentic engine handles the steps; you listen to the result.
- **Dictate longer content on mobile.** Voice is faster than typing on a small screen. Dictate emails, notes, or outlines on iOS or Android and let askROI clean up the prose before you send.
- **Combine voice with Projects.** Open a Project before activating voice so askROI has your full file and history context — responses will be more specific and useful.
- **Adjust playback in noisy environments.** Use headphones for cleaner audio on the go, and make sure your device volume is up before starting playback.

<Tip>
On iOS and Android, you can invoke voice input without touching the screen by using your device's accessibility or shortcut settings — useful for fully hands-free sessions.
</Tip>
