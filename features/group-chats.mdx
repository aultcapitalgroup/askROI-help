Group chats in askROI let you and your teammates work alongside AI together — in the same conversation, with the same context. Whether you're brainstorming ideas, analyzing a shared document, or planning a project, everyone in the chat sees the same thread and gets the same AI-powered assistance.

## What Are Group Chats?

Group chats are shared conversations where multiple people collaborate with AI in one unified space. Unlike a private chat where only you interact with the AI, a group chat gives every participant full visibility into the conversation. The AI maintains shared context across the entire thread, so it understands what each team member has contributed and responds in a way that's relevant to the whole group.

Every message, file, and AI response is visible to all participants the moment it's sent. This means your team stays aligned without anyone needing to copy-paste summaries or catch up in a separate thread.

## Creating a Group Chat

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new chat">
    From the askROI sidebar, click **New Chat**. In the chat creation panel, select **Group Chat** to open the group setup options.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite team members">
    Enter the names or email addresses of the people you want to include. They'll receive an invitation to join the chat — participants need an askROI account to collaborate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a shared context">
    Optionally, add a description or initial prompt to orient the AI toward your team's goal. You can also upload files at this stage so the AI has relevant materials from the start. Then click **Create Group Chat** to open the shared workspace.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How Shared Context Works

When you chat as a group, the AI has access to the full conversation history for every participant — not just the most recent message. This means the AI can reference what your colleague wrote three messages ago, connect it to a file someone uploaded earlier, and give a response that reflects the entire team's input.

The shared context updates in real time. As new messages and files arrive, the AI incorporates them immediately. You don't need to re-explain previous decisions or summarize the discussion — the AI already has the full picture.

<Info>
  Context depth in group chats follows the same limits as individual chats. For long-running projects, use Projects (see below) to maintain persistent context across multiple sessions.
</Info>

## Use Cases

Group chats are well suited for collaborative tasks where multiple perspectives and a shared AI context add real value:

- **Team research** — Ask the AI to search the web, synthesize findings, and answer follow-up questions while your whole team watches and contributes in real time.
- **Collaborative writing** — Draft, review, and refine content together. The AI adapts to feedback from multiple contributors and helps reconcile different writing styles.
- **Project planning** — Discuss goals, break down tasks, and have the AI generate structured plans, timelines, or checklists that the whole team can act on.
- **Shared file analysis** — Upload spreadsheets, PDFs, or documents and let the AI answer questions from any team member about the same source material.

<Tip>
  Pair group chats with Projects to give your team an organized workspace with persistent files, history, and context that carries over between sessions. Projects are especially useful for ongoing team efforts that span multiple conversations.
</Tip>
