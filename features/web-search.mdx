askROI connects directly to the web so your research is never limited to a static knowledge cutoff. Ask any question in chat and askROI retrieves current information, synthesizes it into a clear answer, and cites every source — all without leaving your workspace.

## Searching the Web

To run a web search, simply type your question in the chat input. askROI automatically detects when a query benefits from live results and fetches them in real time. You can also explicitly invoke the search tool by clicking the **Search** icon in the chat toolbar before sending your message.

Once triggered, askROI queries the web, pulls the most relevant pages, and weaves the findings into a coherent response. Because results are fetched in real time, you always get the latest data — stock prices, breaking news, recent research, or anything else that changes frequently.

## Citations and Sources

Every web-search response includes inline citations tied directly to the claims askROI makes. Sources appear as numbered references at the end of each answer, and you can click any citation to open the original page in a new tab.

To review all sources from a response at a glance, look for the **Sources** panel that expands beneath the answer. Each entry shows the page title, domain, and a short excerpt so you can quickly judge credibility before clicking through.

## Live News Briefings

askROI includes a built-in live news feature that surfaces curated, up-to-the-minute headlines from across the web. Open the news briefing from your dashboard or ask askROI to "give me today's top news" in chat to receive a summarized digest with links to the full articles. Use this to stay on top of industry trends, market movements, or any topic you follow closely.

## Extracting Facts from Videos

askROI can pull facts and key information directly from video content. Paste a video URL into the chat and ask a specific question — askROI will analyze the transcript or metadata, extract the relevant details, and present them with a source link back to the original video. This makes it easy to research interviews, lectures, earnings calls, or any video-based content without watching it in full.

<Tip>
  Combine web search with file upload for the most thorough research. Upload a PDF report or spreadsheet, then ask askROI to cross-reference it with live web data — you get internal and external context in a single answer.
</Tip>
