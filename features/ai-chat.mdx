askROI's AI chat goes beyond simple question-and-answer. It learns how you write, remembers your project context, and handles complex, multi-step tasks so you can focus on the work that matters most. Whether you're drafting a report, exploring a research topic, or debugging code, every conversation builds on the last.

## How askROI Learns Your Style

The more you use askROI, the better it understands you. It picks up on your preferred writing tone — casual or formal, concise or detailed — and adjusts its responses to match. Over time it also learns the depth of explanation you prefer, so you're never wading through basics you already know.

askROI adapts to your workflow by remembering which tools you reach for, which projects you return to, and how you like information presented. This context carries across sessions inside a Project, so you never have to re-explain your background or preferences.

<Note>
askROI never stores passwords or sensitive credentials. Context learning applies only to communication style, preferences, and project-specific information you explicitly share.
</Note>

## Starting a Conversation

Open a new chat from the sidebar by clicking **New Chat**. Type your message in the input field and press **Enter** (or tap **Send** on mobile) to get a response.

Within any chat you can reach for built-in tools without leaving the conversation:

- **Web search** — prefix your message with a search request or click the search icon to pull in live, cited results.
- **File upload** — attach a PDF, spreadsheet, or document to give the AI direct access to your content.
- **Image generation** — ask askROI to create or edit an image and it will produce one inline in the chat thread.

<Tip>
You can combine tools in a single message. For example, ask askROI to "upload this PDF, search for the latest industry stats, and write a summary that blends both" — it handles the full sequence for you.
</Tip>

## Chat History

Every conversation is saved automatically. To revisit a previous chat, open the **History** panel in the sidebar and scroll or search for the session you need. Chats are sorted by date and grouped by Project when applicable, so it's easy to find the right context at a glance.

Clicking any past conversation reopens it in full, letting you pick up exactly where you left off, copy previous outputs, or branch into a new direction.

## Agentic Tasks

askROI can execute multi-step tasks autonomously — you describe the goal and it figures out the steps. This means you can hand off complex workflows and return to a finished result rather than managing each stage yourself.

Examples of agentic tasks you can run:

- **Research and report** — "Research the top five competitors in the EV battery market and write a two-page briefing with citations."
- **Web search and synthesis** — "Search for the latest AI regulation news and summarize the key themes into bullet points."
- **File analysis and output** — "Analyze the attached spreadsheet, identify the three highest-cost line items, and draft an executive summary."
- **Code and debugging** — "Review this function, identify bugs, fix them, and explain what you changed."

askROI's 100+ MCP integrations extend agentic capabilities further, connecting to external tools and services so tasks can reach across your entire stack.

<Tip>
Use Projects to keep chat context organized across sessions. When your chat lives inside a Project, askROI automatically pulls in relevant files, history, and background — so every new conversation starts informed.
</Tip>
