If you need to reset your password because you forgot it, follow the instructions outlined on this page.

## From your askROI sign-in page

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter your email">
    Enter your email address on the sign-in page.

    ![askROI sign-in page](https://help.askroi.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Oct-16-2025-10-31-27-3541-PM.png?width=398&height=420&name=image-png-Oct-16-2025-10-31-27-3541-PM.png)

    ![askROI forgot password page](https://help.askroi.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Oct-16-2025-10-33-39-4821-PM.png?width=406&height=336&name=image-png-Oct-16-2025-10-33-39-4821-PM.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Forgot Password">
    Click **Forgot Password**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reset your password">
    Click **Reset your Password**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the security code">
    Retrieve the security code from your email and enter or paste it into the box.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a new password">
    Enter your new password and confirm it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You are now back into your askROI account!
