If you encounter a bug or error in askROI, contact our Customer Support Team so we can help resolve it. This page covers who to reach out to and what to include.

## Who to contact when you receive a bug or an error

**To report any issues, please contact our Customer Support Team.**

- Please provide us with any error messages that you receive. Please provide a screenshot, snips, etc., of these messages and attach them to your email to us at [support@askROI.com](mailto:support@askROI.com).
- If you need to speak to someone in Customer Support, please call us at [888-851-0401](tel:888-851-0401).
