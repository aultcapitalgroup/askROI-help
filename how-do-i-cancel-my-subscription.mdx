---

## First, check where you subscribed

Check your receipt email to see who billed you. Follow the steps for that billing provider.

- **askROI** → [Subscribed on the web](#subscribed-on-the-web)
- **Apple** → [Subscribed on iPhone or iPad](#subscribed-on-iphone-or-ipad)
- **Google Play** → [Subscribed on Android](#subscribed-on-android)

To stop renewal, cancel through your billing provider even if you’ve already deleted the askROI app.

## Subscribed on the web

The workspace owner can open **Manage subscription** in askROI. If you’re a workspace member, ask the owner to cancel the plan.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the account menu">
    Sign in at [askroi.com](https://askroi.com) and click your profile picture in the bottom left corner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manage your subscription">
    Click **Manage subscription**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit feedback or skip">
    In **Why are you leaving?**, choose any reasons that apply and click **Submit feedback**, or click **Skip and continue**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in to the billing portal">
    Enter the email address you used to subscribe and click **Send**. Open the sign-in link in the email to access the billing portal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cancel your plan">
    In your current subscription details, select the option to cancel and follow the prompts. The portal also shows your renewal date, payment method, billing details, and invoice history.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Subscribed on iPhone or iPad

Open your App Store subscriptions using either path.

- **From askROI.** Open the side menu and tap your profile picture at the bottom left. Tap **Manage subscription**, then submit or skip the survey to open your App Store subscriptions.
- **From your device settings.** Open **Settings**, tap your name, then tap **Subscriptions**. Use the Apple Account shown on your receipt.

Tap the askROI subscription, then tap **Cancel Subscription**. If you see an expiration message in red or the cancel button is missing, your subscription is already canceled. See [Apple’s cancellation guide](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118428) for more help.

## Subscribed on Android

Open your Google Play subscriptions using either path.

- **From askROI.** Open the side menu and tap your profile picture at the bottom left. Tap **Manage subscription**, then submit or skip the survey to open your Google Play subscriptions.
- **From Google Play.** Open the **Google Play** app and tap your profile picture. Tap **Payments & subscriptions**, then **Subscriptions**. Use the Google Account you subscribed with.

Tap the askROI subscription, tap **Cancel subscription**, and follow the prompts. See [Google Play’s cancellation guide](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/7018481) for more help.

## If the app says to manage it on the original platform

If you see “Please manage your subscription from the original platform where you purchased it,” follow the steps for [the billing provider on your receipt](#first-check-where-you-subscribed). For example, if you subscribed on the web and see this message on your iPhone, use the askROI billing portal.
