Follow these steps to cancel your Pro Plan subscription. Free accounts do not have a subscription; only Pro Plans do.

For **Free** Accounts, there are no subscriptions set up; only the Pro Plans have a subscription.

To cancel your askROI **Pro** plan subscription, you can either contact our askROI customer support for assistance or follow the steps below to cancel.

You can reach support at [888-851-0401](tel:888-851-0401) for help with canceling your subscription and resolving any related issues, or email us at [support@askROI.com](mailto:support@askROI.com).

## Steps

Log in to your app or account and follow the steps below:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Account">
    Click **Account** (bottom left corner).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Usage">
    Choose **Settings**, then click **Usage**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manage Subscription">
    Click **Manage Subscription**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your email">
    You will be directed to an askROI Billing website. Enter your email address and press **Send**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the email link">
    You will receive an email. Press the **Log in to your customer portal** link.

    ![Login to customer portal email](https://help.askroi.com/hs-fs/hubfs/image-png-Oct-13-2025-10-30-50-1497-PM.png?width=420&height=374&name=image-png-Oct-13-2025-10-30-50-1497-PM.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cancel your subscription">
    Your Current Subscription details will be shown. To the right of the page is where you can cancel your subscription. This is also where you can see your renewal date, update your payment method, update billing information, and see your Invoice History.
  </Step>
</Steps>
