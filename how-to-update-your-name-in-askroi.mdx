Updating your name in askROI takes just a few clicks from the Profile page.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click your Profile Icon (Settings)">
    Select **Settings** from the pop-up menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Profile Settings">
    Click **Profile**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update your name">
    Type your name in the **Full Name** field and press **Update**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your name has been successfully updated in askROI 🎉
