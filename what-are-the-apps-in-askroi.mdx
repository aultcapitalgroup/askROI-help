Under **Apps** within your account, you will find many Apps that you can use for personal and business purposes. This article explains what Apps are and how to use them.

## What are these Apps for and how do I use them?

One example is the **Resume Builder**. The App will give instructions within each field. Once all information is entered, you will press **Generate** at the bottom of the page. Sit back and let the Resume Builder do all the work!

<Note>
  Each App is set up to walk you through the process.
</Note>
