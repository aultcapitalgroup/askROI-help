## Response Times

When you reach out to the askROI team, here's what to expect:

- **Email (Support):** The support team responds to all emails within **one business day**. Include as much detail as possible — your account email, a description of the issue, and any error messages you've seen — so the team can resolve your request without unnecessary back-and-forth.
- **Email (Sales):** The sales team typically responds within **one business day** as well. If you're evaluating askROI for a team or enterprise use case, include the size of your organization and what you're looking to accomplish.
- **Phone:** Both support and sales lines are staffed during **standard business hours (Monday–Friday, 9 AM–5 PM PT)**. If you reach voicemail, leave a message and the team will return your call promptly.
