Whether you're just getting started or looking to make the most of your workspace, these answers cover the questions our users ask most. If you don't find what you're looking for here, reach out to the support team directly.

<Accordion title="What is askROI?">
  askROI is an AI workspace that brings together chat, research, writing, image generation, and file-based work in one place. You can use it on the web, iOS, or Android — everything stays in sync across your devices. askROI adapts to your writing tone and workflow, so the more you use it, the more useful it becomes.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What do I get on the Free plan?">
  The Free plan costs $0/month and includes **2,000 monthly credits**. With those credits you get full access to AI chat, Projects, real-time web search with citations, image generation, file upload and analysis, voice input and playback, and standard support. You can use askROI on the web, iOS, and Android — all at no cost.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What do I get with Pro?">
  The Pro plan is **$10/month** and includes **10,000 monthly credits**. Everything from the Free plan is included, plus higher usage limits across images, web searches, and file analysis, curated playlists, larger Projects with more persistent context, and priority support. Pro is the right choice if you use askROI daily or run larger research and writing workflows.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What are credits?">
  Credits are the currency that powers your activity in askROI. Each action you take — sending a chat message, generating an image, running a web search, or analyzing a file — consumes a small number of credits. More complex or resource-intensive tasks use more credits than simpler ones. Your credit balance resets at the start of each monthly billing cycle.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens when I run out of credits?">
  When your monthly credits are exhausted, you won't be able to start new AI-powered tasks until your balance resets at the beginning of your next billing cycle. If you find yourself running out regularly, upgrading to Pro gives you 10,000 credits per month. You can check your remaining credits at any time in your account settings.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What devices does askROI support?">
  askROI is available on the **web** (any modern browser), **iOS** (iPhone and iPad), and **Android**. Your conversations, Projects, and settings sync automatically across all your devices, so you can start something on your phone and pick it up on your computer without missing a beat.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does askROI store my passwords?">
  No. askROI never stores your passwords. You sign in using your existing **Google**, **Apple**, or **Microsoft** account, or via email — all authentication is handled through those providers' secure systems. askROI does not have access to your credentials.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I cancel or change my plan?">
  You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your plan at any time from your account settings. Navigate to **Settings → Billing** and select the option that fits your needs. If you cancel Pro, you retain access to Pro features until the end of your current billing period, after which your account moves to the Free plan.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I share conversations with my team?">
  Yes. askROI supports **group chats** where multiple people can collaborate with a shared AI context. You can invite teammates to a group chat so everyone sees the same conversation history and AI responses. For larger, ongoing collaboration, Projects give your team a persistent workspace with shared files and history.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is my data private and secure?">
  askROI takes your privacy seriously. Your conversations and files are used to power your personal experience and are not sold to third parties. For the full details on how your data is collected, stored, and protected, review the askROI Privacy Policy linked in the footer of the website.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I get help or support?">
  The fastest way to get help is to email the support team or call directly. You can also browse this documentation for step-by-step guides on every feature. For billing questions, account issues, or anything else, the support team is ready to assist.
</Accordion>
