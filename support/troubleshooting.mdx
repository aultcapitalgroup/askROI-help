Most issues with askROI can be resolved in a few quick steps. Work through the relevant section below, and if the problem persists, the support team is always available to help you get back on track.

## Chat Not Responding

If the AI stops replying or messages aren't sending, try the following steps in order:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check your internet connection">
    askROI requires an active internet connection to process requests. Make sure you're connected to Wi-Fi or mobile data and that other websites or apps are loading normally.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refresh the page or app">
    On web, press **Ctrl+R** (Windows/Linux) or **Cmd+R** (Mac) to reload. On mobile, close and reopen the app. This clears any temporary state that may be blocking your session.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check service status">
    Occasionally askROI may experience brief periods of high demand or maintenance. If the issue seems widespread, check askROI's status page or the official social channels for any active announcements before spending more time troubleshooting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a new conversation">
    If a specific chat thread is unresponsive, try opening a new conversation. If the new chat works normally, the issue is isolated to that thread.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Out of Credits

If you see a message indicating you've run out of credits, here's what to do:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check your remaining credits">
    Go to **Settings → Billing** to see your current credit balance and the date your credits will reset. Free plan users receive 2,000 credits per month; Pro users receive 10,000.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upgrade to Pro">
    If you regularly hit your limit, upgrading to Pro ($10/month) gives you 10,000 monthly credits along with priority support and larger Projects. Select **Upgrade to Pro** in **Settings → Billing** to switch plans instantly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for your monthly reset">
    Credits reset automatically at the start of each billing cycle. If you're close to your reset date, you may prefer to wait rather than upgrade.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## File Upload Issues

If you're having trouble uploading or analyzing a file, check the following:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Verify the file format">
    askROI supports common document and data formats including PDFs, Word documents (.docx), Excel spreadsheets (.xlsx), CSVs, and plain text files. If your file is in a different format, convert it to a supported type before uploading.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the file size">
    Very large files may fail to upload or take longer to process. If your file is unusually large, try splitting it into smaller sections or extracting only the relevant pages before uploading.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Try uploading again">
    Network interruptions during upload can cause failures. Remove the file and try attaching it again. If you're on mobile, switching from cellular to Wi-Fi often helps with larger files.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use copy-paste as a workaround">
    If a file consistently fails to upload, paste the text content directly into the chat. askROI can analyze pasted text just as effectively as uploaded documents.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Sign-In Problems

If you can't sign in to your askROI account, work through these steps:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Clear your browser cache and cookies">
    Stale cookies or cached data can interfere with authentication. In your browser settings, clear cookies and cached files for **askroi.com**, then try signing in again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Try a different sign-in method">
    askROI supports sign-in via **Google**, **Apple**, **Microsoft**, and **email**. If one method isn't working, try another — especially if you may have originally signed up with a different provider.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reset your email password">
    If you signed up with email and can't remember your password, use the **Forgot Password** link on the sign-in page to receive a reset link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Try a different browser or device">
    Browser extensions (such as ad blockers or privacy tools) can sometimes block authentication flows. Try signing in using a private/incognito window or a different browser entirely.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contact support">
    If none of the above resolves the issue, reach out to the support team with the email address associated with your account and a description of the error you're seeing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Integration Connection Issues

If a connected tool or integration stops working or fails to authorize:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Re-authorize the integration">
    Go to **Settings → Integrations**, find the affected tool, and select **Re-authorize** or **Reconnect**. This refreshes the access token without requiring you to fully remove the connection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Disconnect and reconnect">
    If re-authorizing doesn't help, fully disconnect the integration and then reconnect it from scratch. This clears any cached credentials that may have expired or become invalid.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check permissions in the connected app">
    Some integrations require specific permissions within the third-party app (for example, Google Workspace scopes). Make sure askROI has been granted the necessary access in that app's settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check for third-party outages">
    If the connected service itself (e.g., Google Workspace) is experiencing an outage, the integration won't function until that service recovers. Check the third-party provider's status page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Mobile App Issues

If the askROI app on iOS or Android isn't behaving as expected:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Update the app">
    Make sure you're running the latest version of askROI. Open the **App Store** (iOS) or **Google Play** (Android), search for askROI, and install any available updates. askROI v2.0 is 3x faster — keeping the app updated ensures you always get the best performance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check app permissions">
    For voice input to work, askROI needs microphone access. Go to your device's **Settings → Apps → askROI → Permissions** and make sure the microphone (and any other required permissions) are enabled.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Restart the app">
    Close the app completely (swipe it away from your recent apps list) and reopen it. This resolves most temporary glitches without losing any data.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reinstall the app">
    If the app is crashing or consistently misbehaving, uninstall it, restart your device, and reinstall askROI from the App Store or Google Play. Your account data and conversations are stored in the cloud and will be restored when you sign back in.
  </Step>
</Steps>
